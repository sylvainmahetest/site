•VIEW LIVE R&E HARDWARE - SOFTWARE OVERVIEW The research and experiment laboratory is dedicated to the design and fabrication of custom hardware, including programmable controllers and peripherals for a wide range of embedded applications. The laboratory undertakes the design of custom software, from low-level control routines to dedicated embedded programs designed to fully exploit the capabilities of the associated hardware. HARDWARE RESEARCH AND EXPERIMENT The hardware reasearch and experiment division is the department of my laboratory dedicated to mechanics, electronics, and physical and functional testing. My hardware design work consists of multidisciplinary craftsmanship, which corresponds to the areas as outlined in the following sections. ― Mechanical design and manufacturing : • The blueprints are designed from scratch, then the equipment available in the professional workshop, including machine tools, enables the manufacturing and assembly of mechanical parts. ― Electronic design and manufacturing : • The PCB circuit schematics are envisioned and designed to meet dedicated applications. They go through a simulation validation stage, after which the PCBs are designed, manufactured, and assembled. ― Physical and functional testing : • The electronic boards undergo physical testing through electrical, thermal, mechanicals, and functional tests. The fully assembled project is then tested under real-world conditions in suitable environments. SOFTWARE RESEARCH AND EXPERIMENT The software research and experiment is the programming division of my laboratory. My software design work consists of writing libraries and associated programs from scratch, always following the guidelines as outlined below. ― Software consistency : • A syntactic, semantic, and logical nomencature is defined as the standard of consistency to be maintained across all files. ― Functional minimalist : • The code must be as efficient and simple as possible. Validation benchmarks are performed. ― Software independence : • No includes of external files are permitted, in order to garantee portability and prevent long-term obsolescence. ― Programmer simplicity : • On a macroscopic scale, my software libraries are tools that are easy for programmers to use. All complex tasks have already been implemented at the low-level and mid-level layers, and sometimes even at the high-level layer. ― User simplicity : • The user experience takes priority in order to create ergonomic projects for everyone. This is expressed from software to hardware through intuitive and user-friendly human-machine interfaces ― Alternative conceptualization : • Novel algorithms are conceived in order to solve the most complex arithmetic and logical problems. ― Hardware consistency : • The software architecture is consistent with the hardware architecture, while adhering to an electronic nomenclature. palcLibrary.zip •SOFTWARE DOWNLOAD OUTCOMES The many projects that have been conceptualized, built, and tested in real-life settings, along with the public's interest in witnessing live demonstrations, provide opportunities to share ideas and inspire creators who wish to embark on their own research and experimentation.
